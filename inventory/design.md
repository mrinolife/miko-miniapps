# Body Kitchen visual system

## Surface
Operate first, Explore second. The opening screen answers two questions: what needs attention, and what can I make with the energy I have?

## Reference lane
Japanese stationery and kitchen labels crossed with the directness of a good grocery list. Warm uncoated paper, bottle-green ink, restrained amber urgency, editorial serif headings, utilitarian mono labels. This is domestic and tactile rather than futuristic.

## Interaction contract
- Inventory entry must remain useful with only food name and location.
- The use-first queue outranks complete cataloging.
- Every committed item can be used, edited, or removed without hunting through settings.
- Minimum 48px controls in primary flows; persistent bottom navigation; safe-area padding.
- Drafts survive interruption. Camera, barcode, receipt, and voice routes feed one review queue.
- Never use guilt, streak loss, or alarming expiry claims. Dates are confidence-bearing estimates.

## Type
Georgia for compact editorial headings; Avenir Next / Segoe UI for controls and body; system monospace for dates and labels. Native stacks keep the Mini App fast and offline-capable.

## Anti-slop
No gradients, glass cards, floating icon tiles, emoji UI chrome, generic feature grids, fake scores, or shadow-based hierarchy. Use rules, spacing, type, and color blocks.
