# Workout Mode design notes

**Surface:** Operate. This is a live gym console. The next action, exercise state, and skip/resume controls carry the hierarchy.

## Design direction
- Compact dark charcoal interface inspired by industrial training logs and printed gym programs.
- Warm off-white text, safety-orange action color, muted steel secondary controls.
- No gradients, icon-badge clutter, fake metrics, or dashboard filler.
- Large tap targets. The user should be able to operate it while holding a phone between sets.
- Original coach lines: hard-edged, focused, evidence-aware, and concise. They do not imitate a living creator’s language or catchphrases.
